case-manager

case-manager is a private, personal tool used by a single person to organize their own tasks and calendar. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy Policy

What it accesses: with the owner's explicit consent, the app creates, updates and deletes events in the owner's own Google Calendar (scope: calendar.events). It does not read other Google data.

Where data goes: event data is written only to the owner's own Google Calendar. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Storage: the authorization token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private repository and is used only to update that calendar.

Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: November 2026.
